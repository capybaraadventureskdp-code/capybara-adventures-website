STYLE REFERENCE — CAPYBARA WORLD TRAVEL

Create illustrations in a cozy kawaii children's-book style inspired by:
- soft indie storybooks
- Sanrio-like emotional warmth
- collectible travel mascots
- cozy travel journaling aesthetics
- cute sticker-book simplicity
- wholesome children's illustration design

MAIN CHARACTER:
A cute happy capybara mascot used consistently across every illustration.

The capybara should:
- always look emotionally warm and friendly
- have soft rounded shapes
- simple expressive eyes
- a tiny smiling mouth
- blush cheeks
- a gentle wholesome expression
- cozy travel outfits matching the destination
- a plush toy-like appearance
- soft rounded paws
- simple clean facial features
- remain visually recognizable across every image

SIDE CHARACTERS:
Only cute anthropomorphic animals.
Examples:
- rabbits
- cats
- birds
- ducks
- foxes
- pandas
- turtles
- deer

No humans.

STYLE FOR COLORING PAGES:
- black-and-white line art only
- thick clean outlines
- no shading
- no grayscale
- no crosshatching
- no sketch texture
- no messy brush strokes
- no dark fills
- simple uncluttered compositions
- large open coloring spaces
- minimal details
- easy relaxing coloring-book style
- emotionally warm
- cozy and calm
- one clear activity per page
- minimal background clutter
- soft storybook feeling

COMPOSITION RULES FOR COLORING PAGES:
- ONE main focal activity
- lots of white space
- avoid crowded scenes
- avoid too many faces
- avoid excessive objects
- avoid complicated architecture
- avoid tiny unreadable details
- avoid text/signage inside illustrations
- vary the capybara pose and placement each page
- vary camera angles and activities
- simple readable scene layouts

EXAMPLES OF COLORING PAGE COMPOSITIONS:
- capybara waving beside Tokyo Tower
- capybara sipping ramen at a tiny counter
- capybara riding in a gondola
- capybara holding a camera under cherry blossoms
- capybara wearing a scarf in Iceland
- capybara drinking coffee in NYC
