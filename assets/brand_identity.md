Studying the Tokyo, NYC, and Venice cover pages reveals:

1. **Wide rectangular-ish head** — clearly wider than a bear's round head. Almost square.

# CHARACTER DESIGN SPECIFICATION

## Species

Capybara

---

## Body Shape

Rounded bean-shaped body. Large soft torso. Short thick legs. Small rounded paws. Compact proportions. Extremely cuddly silhouette. No sharp angles. No aggressive shapes. Every body part should be based on circles, ovals, and rounded curves.

---

## Head Shape

Large oversized head. Head approximately 40–45% of total character height. Wide rounded cheeks. Rounded muzzle. Soft forehead. No angular jawline. No sharp snout.

---

## Eyes

Large kawaii eyes. Circular or slightly oval. Wide spacing. Friendly expression. Eyes should communicate warmth and curiosity. No realistic capybara eyes. No tiny bead eyes. No anime eyes with excessive detail. Keep eyes simple and iconic.

Eyes should ALWAYS:
* be black or very dark brown
* contain 1–2 simple white highlights
* remain large and rounded
* stay vertically compact
* avoid realistic eye anatomy

Avoid: eyelashes, detailed irises, anime sparkle overload, sleepy droopy eyes, realistic animal eyes.

Recommended consistency phrase: "large rounded black kawaii eyes with simple white highlights"

---

## Eyebrows

Tiny expressive eyebrows. Used frequently for emotion. Help communicate: wonder, excitement, happiness, curiosity, gratitude.

---

## Nose

Small rounded triangular nose. Always centered. Simple shape.

---

## Mouth

Tiny simple smile. Never exaggerated. Never large teeth. Never open screaming mouth. Occasionally tiny "o" shape for surprise.

---

## Ears

Small rounded ears. Placed near top corners of head. Simple clean shape.

---

## Fur

Smooth. Minimal texture. No individual hair rendering. No realistic fur simulation. No furry detailing. Character should look plush-like.
