Who is Capy?

Capy is an adorable anthropomorphic capybara with a warm golden tan coat, a soft rounded pear-shaped head, and the most expressive eyes you've ever seen on a rodent.


Armed with a camera, a paw-print suitcase, and an endless appetite for local food, Capy travels the world collecting memories, making animal friends, and finding joy in every destination.


In every city, Capy wears a local-inspired outfit — a floral kimono in Tokyo, an NYC hoodie in New York, a striped gondolier shirt in Venice — but the warm smile and blush cheeks never change.

The Series

A Global Travel Adventure

Each Capybara Adventures book follows Capy through a full trip to a new destination — from the exciting airport arrival to the bittersweet farewell, with 25+ unique coloring pages in between.


Every page features a different scene, a different landmark, a different food, and a different pose — no two pages look alike. Companion animals join Capy throughout each adventure, adding life and personality to every spread.


The series is designed to be collected. Each book is a standalone adventure, but together they form a cozy world of travel, culture, and friendship.
